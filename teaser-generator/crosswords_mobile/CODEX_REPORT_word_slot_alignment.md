### Sorting rules for Word 1..5
1. Derive all start cells from each slotâ€™s first grid coordinate and assign ascending clue numbers by scanning row-major (top-to-bottom, then left-to-right) so across/down at the same cell share the same number.

3. After sorting, the display index (`Word N`) is simply the slotâ€™s position in this list.
